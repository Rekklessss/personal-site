Built a native Swift app for following esports events, live scores, match statistics, and personalized news. Integrated third-party REST APIs using URLSession and Combine, with attention to pagination, resilient networking, and a responsive mobile interface.

I built Gamergrid during the Apple and Infosys iOS App Development Program. It brought my experience in competitive esports together with native app development and practical networking.

[Explore the source and documentation](https://github.com/Rekklessss/Gamergrid).
