Developed a geospatial assistant prototype combining retrieval over reference material, language-model interaction, and GIS tooling. Explored how to connect natural-language requests with spatial data and tools while keeping analysis steps inspectable.

This is an experimental application rather than a production inference system. The work gave me experience coordinating retrieval, spatial data sources, and tools around a language-model interface.

[Explore the source and documentation](https://github.com/Rekklessss/geogpt-rag).
