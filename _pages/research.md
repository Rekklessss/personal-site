**Divyanshu Pabia, Manasvi Rao Kanukolan, and A. Anbarasi.** “A Decentralized Blockchain Framework for Secure, Transparent and Privacy-Preserving Toll Payment Systems.” _2025 International Conference on Smart & Sustainable Technology (INCSST)_, July 2025. [DOI: 10.1109/INCSST64791.2025.11210370](https://doi.org/10.1109/INCSST64791.2025.11210370).

The paper proposes an electronic tolling architecture that combines RFID events, Ethereum account abstraction, sponsored transactions, and zero-knowledge authentication. It examines how an auditable payment flow might reduce reliance on a central clearinghouse while protecting vehicle identity. It is a research proposal and prototype, not a deployed tolling system.
